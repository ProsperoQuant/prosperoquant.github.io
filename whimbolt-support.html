Whimbolt: Screw Puzzle

A 3D workshop puzzle about restoring clockwork objects. Turn each mechanism, remove and sort its screws, and reveal the artifact hidden inside. Wind completed mechanisms again from the collection.

Workshop orders

The first two orders are free. An optional reward video unlocks Celesta, the third order, after the ad grants its reward. The one-time unlock pack grants orders 3–7 and turns off advertising. The in-game shop displays the price from the App Store for your region.

Restore purchases

Open the shop from the main menu and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally bought the pack.

Advertising and privacy

Whimbolt uses Google AdMob for optional reward videos. If privacy choices are required for your region, the shop offers access to those settings. Google explains how it uses data from partner apps.
