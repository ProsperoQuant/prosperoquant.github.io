Peekweave Support

Explore hand-painted worlds and find what the scenery hides. Peekweave is coming to iPhone and iPad.

Finding objects

Use the object strip to see what is missing. Pinch to zoom and look along the edges of furniture, foliage, and shadows. Watch the moving parts of a scene. In some locations a swinging light, curtain, or another animation reveals an object for a short time, then brings it back into view.

Restoring Remove Ads

Open the Shop and select Restore Purchases. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account that made the purchase. The verified purchase turns off interstitial ads.

Contact support

. Include your app version, device model, and the location name if the issue occurs in a scene.
