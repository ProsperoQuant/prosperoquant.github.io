Find 832 hidden objects in 64 illustrated locations across eight worlds on iPhone and iPad. Follow a railway beneath the sea, explore a lantern-lit café, and step into a snowy Christmas carriage.

Peekweave on the App Store · Privacy policy

Finding objects

Use the object strip to see what is missing. Pinch to zoom and look along the edges of furniture, foliage, and shadows. Watch the moving parts of a scene. In some locations a swinging light, curtain, or another animation reveals an object for a short time, then brings it back into view.

Progress and settings

Your progress stays on your device. Settings lets you change the language, adjust music and sounds, reduce animations, or reset progress in all worlds. Peekweave is available in English, Russian, Spanish, German, and French.

Restore or manage a subscription

In the Shop, choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that started the annual Ad-free subscription. Choose Manage Subscription to change or cancel renewal with Apple. If you cancel, your access lasts until the end of the paid period.

Contact support

. Include your app version, device model, and the location name if the issue occurs in a scene.
