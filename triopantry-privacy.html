TrioPantry Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026
Developer: Andrii Kolmohorov / ProsperoQuant
Contact:

Local game data

TrioPantry is a shelf-sorting game and does not require an account. Progress, saved games, discoveries, scores, language and sound preferences are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives your game progress. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your backup settings.

Advertising and information processed by Google

TrioPantry includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). It can show interstitial advertisements between games and optional rewarded videos. A rewarded video provides temporary access in the game, not a purchase or a cash reward. Google and advertising partners may process the IP address and approximate location derived from it; device identifiers, including the advertising identifier when permitted; advertisement impressions, video views and other interactions; and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. These data support advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention and service operation. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

iOS advertising configuration

Personalized advertising and Google’s publisher first-party identifier are enabled only when the required Google advertising purposes are granted and Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization is allowed. The app can ask Apple’s system tracking question after Google’s privacy flow when advertising consent permits it. Apple controls whether that question can be displayed. Without both permissions, the app requests non-personalized ads and disables publisher privacy personalization and the publisher first-party identifier. The app disables automatic device-location collection through the advertising SDK control and does not ask for precise device location; approximate location can still be inferred from an IP address. Advertising may be limited or unavailable depending on your privacy choices.

Privacy choices and withdrawal

The European Google UMP consent message is configured for the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, not worldwide. A separate Google message provides sale, sharing and applicable targeted-advertising choices in US states supported by Google’s state-privacy system. Region is determined by Google; the language of the game does not select a legal region. A previously saved decision may remain valid after a change to message targeting.

Google UMP provides privacy choices before eligible advertising requests when a form is required by its configuration. The applicable choices can be reopened from Settings > Ad privacy choices, and from the advertising privacy choices in the shop, when available. In supported US states, use this entry to access Google’s “Do not sell or share” / opt-out choices. You can change or withdraw the choices made there; Google stores the applicable choice for subsequent launches. Apple tracking permission is managed separately in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or through the link to iOS settings in TrioPantry Settings. The app checks Google’s advertising eligibility and Apple’s permission rather than treating installation, continued play or acceptance of this policy as consent. Refusing or withdrawing an optional choice does not delete progress or purchased content and does not prevent playing the free levels. Some ads or optional video rewards may then be unavailable. Resetting game progress is not a way of withdrawing consent or deleting data already held by Google.

The current advertising-partner list and each partner’s stated purposes are available in Google’s message. Google maintains a default partner list, which may change; its displayed count is not a count of SDKs installed in TrioPantry or a statement that every listed partner receives each player’s data. The app currently integrates Google Mobile Ads and UMP without third-party mediation SDKs. Google’s standard message can describe partners’ possible use of precise geolocation. TrioPantry does not request or supply precise device coordinates; the app’s actual location configuration is described above.

Firebase and game analytics

The first release does not include or initialize the Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Performance SDKs and does not send gameplay events, Firebase crash reports or Firebase performance traces. Development work for these features and a Firebase project linked to AdMob are retained for a possible future update. Linking the consoles does not activate Firebase SDK collection in the app. AdMob continues its own advertising analytics and diagnostic processing described above. Any later activation of Firebase collection will require an app update, updated privacy information and applicable privacy choices.

Purchases

Purchases, where available in a released version, are processed by the platform store. TrioPantry does not receive payment card details. Verified store transaction information is used locally to recognize and restore purchased content and handle revocations. Store-managed purchase information is subject to the platform’s own policy. The current development configuration does not enable production checkout.

Permissions and third-party content

TrioPantry does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise device location. Opening an advertisement or external website leads to third-party content governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. This policy covers TrioPantry, not external sites.

Retention, international processing and your rights

Local game data remains on the device until reset or removal of the app, subject to operating-system backups. Google and Apple determine retention for information they process under their respective policies. Their services may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, using applicable transfer arrangements. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict processing; or opt out of certain sales, sharing or targeted advertising. Use the contact button on this page for requests concerning the developer. You may also exercise rights directly with Google or Apple for information they hold. Withdrawing consent stops applicable future processing; it does not by itself erase previously processed data. The developer does not maintain a player account or a server-side gameplay profile with which to identify individual players.

Children

TrioPantry is intended for a general audience, is not designed specifically for children, and is not offered as an Apple Kids Category app. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact the address below. Platform parental controls may restrict advertisements, purchases or access to the app.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app or its services change. Contact the developer and privacy contact, Andrii Kolmohorov, using the email button on this page. The effective date is shown above.
