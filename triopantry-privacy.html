TrioPantry Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026
Developer: Andrii Kolmohorov / ProsperoQuant. Contact: prosperoprofit@gmail.com.

TrioPantry is a shelf-sorting game. It does not require an account. Game progress, saved games, discoveries, scores, sound preferences and language are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives your game progress. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your own backup settings.

Advertising and privacy choices
The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show interstitial advertising between games and optional rewarded advertising. An earned reward provides temporary access within the app; watching an ad does not constitute a purchase. The iOS app requests non-personalized advertising, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier and publisher privacy personalization, disables automatic device-location collection by the advertising SDK, and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization or access to the IDFA. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

As described by Google, its advertising SDK may process IP address and approximate location derived from it; app/device identifiers; advertising impressions, video views and other interactions; crash logs and performance information. Google uses this information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention and service operation. This information is processed by Google and its advertising partners under their policies. No separate Firebase Analytics or third-party crash reporting service is included in this app.

Where applicable, Google's consent form presents privacy options before advertisements are requested. You can revisit required advertising choices from the app's advertising privacy options. Refusing a choice does not delete your game progress; some advertisements or optional rewards may be unavailable. The app does not require consent for personalized advertising in order to play the free levels. UMP stores and communicates privacy choices as necessary to apply them. Privacy rights and available choices depend on your region.

Purchases
Any in-app purchases are processed by the platform store. TrioPantry does not receive your payment card details. The app uses verified store transaction information to recognize and restore purchased content and handle revocations. Purchase history managed by Apple is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions and third-party content
TrioPantry does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise device location. Opening an advertisement or an external website takes you to third-party content governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy. This policy covers TrioPantry, not those external services.

Retention and control
Local game data remains on the device until you reset progress or remove the app, subject to operating-system backups. Resetting progress does not cancel a store purchase or erase Google's consent records. Use the in-app privacy controls to change applicable advertising choices. Google and Apple manage retention of data they process under their respective policies. For questions or applicable privacy-rights requests, contact the developer at the address above; you may also exercise rights directly with Google or Apple for data held by them.

Children
TrioPantry is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact the address above so the request can be reviewed. Device and platform parental controls can restrict advertisements, purchases or access to the app.

Changes and contact
This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The current policy will display its effective date. Contact: prosperoprofit@gmail.com.
