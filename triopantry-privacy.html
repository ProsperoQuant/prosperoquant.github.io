TrioPantry Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026
Developer: Andrii Kolmohorov / ProsperoQuant
Contact: prosperoprofit@gmail.com

Local game data

TrioPantry is a shelf-sorting game and does not require an account. Progress, saved games, discoveries, scores, language and sound preferences are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives your game progress. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your backup settings.

Advertising and information processed by Google

TrioPantry includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). It can show interstitial advertisements between games and optional rewarded videos. A rewarded video provides temporary access in the game, not a purchase or a cash reward. Google and advertising partners may process the IP address and approximate location derived from it; app- or developer-bounded device identifiers; advertisement impressions, video views and other interactions; and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. These data support advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention and service operation. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

iOS advertising configuration

The iOS app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier and publisher privacy personalization, and disables automatic device-location collection through the advertising SDK control. The latter does not prevent approximate location being inferred from an IP address. The app does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency authorization or read the IDFA. It does not ask for precise device location. These settings do not mean that all device identifiers or all advertising measurement are absent.

Privacy choices and withdrawal

Where required, Google UMP provides privacy choices before eligible advertising requests. The applicable choices can be reopened from Settings > Advertising privacy options, and from the advertising privacy options in the shop, when those options are required for your region. You can change or withdraw the choices made there. The app checks Google’s advertising eligibility rather than treating installation, continued play or acceptance of this policy as consent. Refusing or withdrawing an optional choice does not delete progress or purchased content and does not prevent playing the free levels. Some ads or optional video rewards may then be unavailable. Resetting game progress is not a way of withdrawing consent or deleting data already held by Google.

Firebase and game analytics

A Firebase project has been created for TrioPantry and linked to AdMob. In the current iOS app configuration, the Firebase Analytics SDK is not integrated or initialized, and the app does not send separate gameplay analytics events to Firebase. Linking the consoles alone does not add that collection to the app. Google may still use data collected by its advertising SDK for advertising analytics as described above. Any later introduction of Firebase app analytics will be reflected in this policy and the App Store privacy information before release, with applicable privacy choices applied.

Purchases

Purchases, where available in a released version, are processed by the platform store. TrioPantry does not receive payment card details. Verified store transaction information is used locally to recognize and restore purchased content and handle revocations. Store-managed purchase information is subject to the platform’s own policy. The current development configuration does not enable production checkout.

Permissions and third-party content

TrioPantry does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise device location. Opening an advertisement or external website leads to third-party content governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. This policy covers TrioPantry, not external sites.

Retention, international processing and your rights

Local game data remains on the device until reset or removal of the app, subject to operating-system backups. Google and Apple determine retention for information they process under their respective policies. Their services may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, using applicable transfer arrangements. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict processing; or opt out of certain sales, sharing or targeted advertising. Contact prosperoprofit@gmail.com for requests concerning the developer. You may also exercise rights directly with Google or Apple for information they hold. Withdrawing consent stops applicable future processing; it does not by itself erase previously processed data. The developer does not maintain a player account or a server-side gameplay profile with which to identify individual players.

Children

TrioPantry is intended for a general audience, is not designed specifically for children, and is not offered as an Apple Kids Category app. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact the address below. Platform parental controls may restrict advertisements, purchases or access to the app.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app or its services change. Contact the developer and privacy contact, Andrii Kolmohorov, at prosperoprofit@gmail.com. The effective date is shown above.
