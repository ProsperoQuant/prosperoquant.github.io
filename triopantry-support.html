TrioPantry Support

A cozy shelf-sorting puzzle. For help, contact prosperoprofit@gmail.com.

How to play

Tap or drag objects to gather three matching objects on one shelf. The Dresses theme uses pairs. Each level explains its special rule before play. Choose Relaxed mode for a game without a timer, or Timed mode for a challenge.

Levels and purchases

The first 20 levels in every theme are free and work offline. Permanent theme continuations cover levels 21–50. The complete collection covers all 19 current themes and removes automatic advertising breaks. The separate Remove Ad Breaks purchase removes automatic breaks across the game, but does not unlock paid continuations. These are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. Prices and availability are provided by the store.

Restore purchases

Open the Shop and choose Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. An internet connection is required. If a purchase is pending, content opens only after the store confirms it. Contact support if a confirmed purchase cannot be restored. Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or authentication codes.

Optional video access

When available, a voluntary rewarded video provides temporary access to five levels in the selected theme. This is not a permanent purchase. If a video cannot load, free levels and owned content remain playable.

Progress and settings

Progress is stored on the device. Deleting the app or choosing Reset Progress may remove local progress. Music, sound effects, reduced motion, and language can be changed in Settings.

Privacy choices

Open Settings and choose the advertising privacy option to review or change the choices available in your region. Refusing consent does not prevent ordinary gameplay. Apple's tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance collection are disabled in the first release.
